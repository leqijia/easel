Easel support

Easel for iPad and Easel Display for Mac

Showing the board on your Mac

  1. Open Easel Display on the Mac. It shows a six-digit code.
  2. In Easel on the iPad, open a project and tap Connect to Mac.
  3. Choose your Mac and enter the code.

Both devices need the same Wi-Fi network with Bluetooth on. The first time, each asks for Local Network access; tap Allow. If you declined, turn it on in Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on the iPad, and System Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network on the Mac.

If the Mac doesn’t appear, some school and guest networks stop devices from seeing each other. A personal hotspot or a home network will work.

Recording a lesson

Tap Record. Easel records the whole page and your voice, whatever your zoom. Tap the timer to pause, and the square to stop. Recordings are kept in each project; tap the recordings button to play, share or delete them.

Page shape and recording quality

Choose them when you create a project, or later from the menu › Page setup. Writing outside a smaller page is hidden, not deleted.
