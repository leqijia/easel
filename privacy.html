Privacy policy

Easel and Easel Display · Updated October 14, 2026

Easel and Easel Display don’t collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your devices

Your projects, pages and recordings are saved only on your iPad. Recordings include microphone audio, which Easel uses only while you are recording, and only to make the recording on your iPad.

The connection to your Mac

When you connect to a Mac, the board is sent directly from the iPad to the Mac over your local network or Bluetooth, encrypted, and protected by the six-digit code. It does not pass through any server, and Easel Display doesn’t save it.

Sharing

Recordings and PDFs leave your iPad only when you share them yourself, for example with AirDrop or Messages.
